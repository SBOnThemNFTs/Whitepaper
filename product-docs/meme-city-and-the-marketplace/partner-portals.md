# Partner Portals

In Meme City a player doesn't scroll past a partner. They walk into it. Partner Portals are branded buildings on the streets players already use: a custom storefront, a logo hovering over the roof, a door that is a portal. Step through and a short branded clip plays as you're carried to the partner's site, store, token page or community. It's the difference between an impression and a visit.

## Already On The Map

The city has working portals to the tools every Solana trader lives in: Phantom, Dexscreener, Jupiter, Meteora, Moonshot, Orbis and Play Solana, so a player can go from the street to a chart, a swap or a wallet in one step. The Marketplace has its own portal building with a round trip back into the same session. Reserved "Coming Soon" lots are waiting for the next partners.

*Portals link to public sites; named companies are not affiliated with Meme Theft Auto unless a partnership is announced.*

## Why The Biggest Names In Solana Want A Building Here

Meme City's audience is the most qualified in crypto: every player arrives with a Solana wallet, in a 30-minute session, not a three-second scroll. A Phantom portal puts the wallet one doorway from the player who just earned a round's cash. A Dexscreener portal sits exactly where a trader checks a chart mid-session. A Jupiter or Meteora portal is a swap one step from the street. For a partner, that is persistent, walk-in placement in a city built for the people they're trying to reach.

## What It Does For MTA And $MTA Holders

Short term, every partner that takes a building brings its own audience with it: a Phantom, Dexscreener or Jupiter community discovering Meme City is new players, new wallets and new eyes on $MTA on the very platforms where traders find tokens. Partner placements are paid in $MTA, so each signing is demand for the token on day one.

Long term, portal and ad revenue is recurring income the project earns in $MTA rather than by selling it, revenue the Treasury keeps under its published no-sell policy. Frontage in the city is finite, so as traffic grows, the value of each lot grows with it. More partners means more reasons to be in the city, more players means more partners want in, and the token that prices every placement sits in the middle of that loop.

## Who It's For

Web3 projects and token communities, retail brands, music artists, X influencers, and streamers, bloggers and vloggers who want their audience somewhere to gather that isn't a feed.

## How It Runs

The MTA team builds every portal and produces its entry clip through the game's admin panel; partners don't upload their own assets, which keeps every building to the same quality bar and keeps the game fast. Placements are paid in $MTA.
