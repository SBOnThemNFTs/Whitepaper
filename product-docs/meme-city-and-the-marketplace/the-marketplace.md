# The Marketplace

The Marketplace at [marketplace.memetheftauto.com](https://marketplace.memetheftauto.com) is where $MTA turns into things you can hold. It is Meme Theft Auto's official store, and everything in it is paid in $MTA only, from any Solana wallet. No fiat. No card checkout. No exceptions.

## Made For MTA, Nowhere Else

Nothing in the store is stock merch with a logo slapped on. Each piece is a custom MTA edition designed with a maker and released as a numbered drop: parfums, neon posters, rugs, leather goods, a branded Ledger hardware wallet, with new editions landing on a regular cadence. When a drop sells out it is gone; it is never restocked as a generic product. Each item shows its $MTA price with the USD equivalent, so the price in dollars holds steady while the amount of $MTA it takes moves with the market.

## Tiers: The Deepest Holders Get First Pick

Every drop is marked with a holder tier, set by the $MTA balance in the connected wallet.

| Tier | $MTA held |
| ---- | --------- |
| T1   | 1,000,000 or more |
| T2   | 500,000 or more |
| T3   | 250,000 or more |
| T4   | at least 1 $MTA |

The rarest pieces are T1 only; the more you hold, the more of the store opens to you. Holding is the only way in, and selling down closes doors, which is exactly the point.

## Why $MTA-Only Is The Whole Point

Every purchase is $MTA that a buyer had to acquire from the trading pool first, so the store is a steady source of buying that has nothing to do with speculation: it exists because people want the products. The project never sells $MTA for fiat, and the Marketplace Reserve is spent only on fulfilment, never sold. Makers are paid in $MTA from that reserve; a maker who prefers cash is paid from the pool's trading fees, and the $MTA from that sale is split 50% to the Treasury and 50% burned. So each sale either passes tokens to a maker who now holds them, or removes them from circulation forever. Demand goes up, supply goes down, and the project adds no sell pressure of its own. That is the structure behind price stability, and it compounds with every drop.

## For Partners And Makers

A drop in the Marketplace puts a product in front of a buying audience that already holds the currency, inside a game that sends players through a portal straight to the shelf. Makers are paid in $MTA on vesting terms wherever possible, so our partners become holders, not sellers. Full mechanics: [Marketplace Reserve 15%](../usdmta-token/tokenomics/marketplace-reserve-15.md).

## Beta And $bMTA

Until $MTA lists on Nov 21, 2026, the store runs in beta and prices show in $bMTA, a test token used only to exercise checkout. At listing, $bMTA is retired and every price switches to $MTA at the same USD value.
