# What Is Meme Theft Auto?

Meme Theft Auto is a system to reward existing GTA Online players retroactively based around 1 metric - _How much in-game cash has been earned through the life of a player’s account_.  We’ve developed a platform to identify player profiles, isolate the key metric, and authenticate ownership of that profile. This process has given us the ability to produce an effective system to properly reward players based off their commitment. The most feasible way of execution would be through distribution of a memecoin… $MTA. \
\
Meme Theft Auto is aimed to capture the essence of what the meme coin market really is. Some may call it the trenches, others may refer to it as the Wild West. Either way, the meme coin trading world can be brutal, strictly every man for himself, and unforgiving to rushed decisions, mistakes, and careless actions.&#x20;

Much like the video game phenomenon Grand Theft Auto V, there is only one real objective, to make money. There may be millions of actions in between, but it all leads back to acquiring as much money as possible. With a user base of over 500 Million players worldwide, the accumulated virtual cash earned in game has exceeded $125 Trillion Dollars, and growing every second. So much so, that there has been widespread speculation that GTA creators _Rockstar_, may be implementing cryptocurrency into the long anticipated successor of GTA V… GTA 6. The aim would be to reward GTA6 players with actual IRL (In Real Life) currency, based on the amount of money earned during online gameplay. This would transform an activity mainly used for mere pleasure, into an actual source of income. Such a decision would be a monumental shift for the gaming world, bring retired gamers back to their joysticks, creating a new wave of entrepreneurs, and bringing crypto and memecoin even further into the financial spotlight.&#x20;

Out of all of the anticipation…..a question arose, and a thought developed.&#x20;

### -_What about the players that have already put in countless hours of gameplay and assembled virtual fortunes? Aren’t they entitled to a reward for their long term commitment?_ <br>
