# Meme City

Meme City is the first place a memecoin community can actually hang out. It's a browser-based multiplayer sandbox at [memecity.memetheftauto.com](https://memecity.memetheftauto.com): no download, no sign-up. Pick a character and you're on the street in seconds, on a phone or a desktop, in a shared city with up to 12 players, or a private city for your own crew.

## The Game

Every session is a 30-minute round in a living city: civilians, traffic, gangs, police. Earn cash, arm up at the gun shop, drive off the dealership lot in anything from an e-scooter to a limo, a tank or a UFO, pick up wanted stars and shake the cops, grab a meal to heal, take a helicopter or a boat out over the water. Then do it again with a bigger plan.

## Every Street Is A Doorway

Storefronts across the city are portals. Walk into the Marketplace and a cinematic transition carries you into the MTA store; come back within five minutes and you're dropped into the same session with your cash, weapons and wanted level intact. Walk into a partner's building and you're on their site. Billboards downtown, on the beachfront, in the industrial district and along the waterfront, bus-stop posters, storefront strips and car wraps are all live ad surfaces. Meme City is a game first, and the best-converting media in Web3 second. See [Partner Portals](partner-portals.md).

## Built To Expand

The city is designed to keep growing without ever slowing down. New districts modelled on real cities bolt on as branch-off areas that never overlap the main map. Characters, vehicles and storefronts are added through the admin panel between rounds, so the roster grows every week. Interiors are already in: the lobby opens onto five portal doors, with more interiors on the way. The rule for every addition is the same: if it costs frame rate on a phone, it doesn't ship.

## Where It Goes Next

* Themed districts: a casino strip, a heist district
* Ranked rounds and tournaments with $MTA prizes
* Organized community events with their own private cities
* Seasonal drops tied to the Marketplace
* X Spaces hosted live inside the city
* Partner- and community-run events on the map

Each one adds a reason to be in Meme City, and every reason to be in the city is a reason to hold the token that runs it.

## The $MTA Loop

$MTA is Meme City's currency. Partner placements are bought in $MTA, the Marketplace inside the city takes only $MTA, and player rewards and premium content are designed to run through it. In-game cash resets each round; $MTA is what persists, which keeps the fun free to try and the value in the token.
