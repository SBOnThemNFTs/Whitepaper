# Presale Vesting & Round Multipliers

Presale tokens are delivered through the presale contract on a per‑round schedule. The rule is _discount buys time_: the cheaper the round, the slower its tokens reach the market.

<table><thead><tr><th>Round</th><th>Unlocked at listing</th><th width="265">Remainder</th><th>Fully unlocked</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>1</td><td>20%</td><td>Streams evenly over 120 days</td><td>Day 120</td></tr><tr><td>2</td><td>35%</td><td>Streams evenly over 90 days</td><td>Day 90</td></tr><tr><td>3</td><td>50%</td><td>Streams evenly over 60 days</td><td>Day 60</td></tr></tbody></table>

> Nothing is claimable before listing. Streams run continuously — there is no waiting for a monthly cliff.

**Value at listing.** With the presale fully subscribed, the pool opens at $0.00667. Measured against what each round paid:

| Round | Paid    | Listing price | Value at listing |
| ----- | ------- | ------------- | ---------------- |
| 1     | $0.0010 | $0.00667      | 6.7×             |
| 2     | $0.0030 | $0.00667      | 2.2×             |
| 3     | $0.0040 | $0.00667      | 1.7×             |

If the presale closes early with less raised, the pool is sized so the same pattern holds: at $400,000 raised, Round 1 lists at 4.0× and Round 2 at 1.33×; at $100,000, Round 1 lists at 1.25×.

**Why the schedule is fair to every round.** Round 3 buyers paid the most and get the fastest access: half their tokens the moment trading opens, the rest within two months, at a listing price 1.7× what they paid. Round 1 buyers paid the least and hold the largest paper gain, so their tokens arrive slowest — which is exactly what protects the price for Rounds 2 and 3. On listing day 130M of the 350M presale tokens are unlocked; the rest arrive over four months, cheapest last, at a combined pace of roughly 2.6M tokens a day — under 2% of the pool. Ordinary demand absorbs that. A single‑day free‑for‑all would not be absorbed by anything.

**If you buy in more than one round.** Each token keeps the schedule of the round it was bought in, so a multi‑round buyer's unlock is automatically the blend of those schedules — never the sum of them, and never the fastest one applied to everything. For buyers who take the full limit in each round:

| ------------- | ---------- | ------------------- | ------ | ------ | ------ | ------- |

| 2 + 3         | 2,625,000  | 44%                 | 68%    | 92%    | 100%   | —       |
| 1 + 3         | 2,125,000  | 43%                 | 67%    | 91%    | 95%    | 100%    |
| 1 + 2         | 1,500,000  | 30%                 | 51%    | 72%    | 93%    | 100%    |
| 3 only        | 1,625,000  | 50%                 | 75%    | 100%   | —      | —       |
| 2 only        | 1,000,000  | 35%                 | 57%    | 78%    | 100%   | —       |
| 1 only        | 500,000    | 20%                 | 40%    | 60%    | 80%    | 100%    |

The table shows the largest possible position at each step: the biggest buyer in the presale can have 40% of their tokens available on listing day and nothing more, and nobody's vesting is longer than 120 days.

> _Listing price is set by the pool ratio on listing day. Market price after listing is set by trading and is not guaranteed._
