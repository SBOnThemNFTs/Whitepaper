What the table shows: on listing day, 97% of what can circulate is either presale buyers or the permanently locked pool — the project's own allocations are 1% of supply. The two allocations spent on the project (Marketplace Reserve and Treasury) are the slowest to arrive, and the Treasury can't be touched for a quarter. Burns from unsold presale tokens, unused pool tokens, unclaimed rewards and marketplace sales reduce every number above permanently.

## What this means for you

**If you're in the presale:** you know your price, your limit, your unlock schedule and the listing price formula before you deposit. You know the pool can't be pulled. You know the community's free tokens arrive one‑sixth at a time, and no allocation the project controls can be sold faster than a tenth of weekly volume.

**If you're claiming:** your rate and price are fixed, you can verify whenever you're ready during the six months, your claim unlocks at the same pace as everyone else's, and the tokens you claim can only get scarcer — unclaimed tokens are burned, not redistributed.

**If you're buying on the market:** every schedule above is on‑chain and verifiable, the trading pool is permanent, and the marketplace turns every purchase into demand or a burn.

Short‑term, the design keeps listing day orderly. Long‑term, it keeps supply predictable, keeps the pool deep and permanent, and makes every burn — unsold, unused, unclaimed, and every marketplace sale — one‑way.
