# The Presale

The presale is the only time $MTA is sold at a fixed price, and the only time it is sold below the pool it lists into. It is live at [presale.memetheftauto.com](https://presale.memetheftauto.com): opened Oct 27, 2026 at 11:59 PM Eastern, closes Nov 19, 2026 at 11:59 PM Eastern or earlier if Round 3 sells out, and $MTA lists on Nov 21, 2026 at 12:00 PM Eastern. 100% of what is raised goes into a trading pool that is locked permanently at listing: nobody, including the team, can ever pull it. Round 1 buyers pay $0.001 against a fully subscribed listing price of $0.00667; the earlier you are, the more the pool opens above what you paid, and the published vesting means no round can dump on another.

Rounds open in sequence: Round 2 opens when Round 1 sells out, Round 3 when Round 2 sells out. The $10,000 cap across all rounds is per buyer, not per wallet.

## How To Buy

1. Hold USDC on Solana in Phantom, Solflare or Backpack, plus a little SOL for fees. Need USDC? Swap on [jup.ag](https://jup.ag/swap/SOL-USDC) first.
2. On the buy page, connect your wallet and enter an amount. On your first purchase in a round the presale server checks the wallet and sets its limit; this takes a few seconds and needs no sign-up.
3. Approve one transaction in your wallet. The page shows you exactly what it does before the wallet opens.
4. After listing, come back to the claim page. Part of your tokens unlock at listing and the rest stream over the vesting period.

## Before You Buy

* Purchases are final. The vault program has withdrawals disabled on-chain.
* Brand-new wallets are refused; use one with some history.
* Nothing is claimable until all three rounds end. The unlock schedule is written into each vault and cannot be changed afterwards.
* The sale runs only from presale.memetheftauto.com, and the team never messages first. We never ask for your seed phrase, private key, or a wallet "validation".
