# Treasury 5%

The Treasury backs development, partnerships, community initiatives and unforeseen needs. It is controlled by the founder, who is accountable for every use of it, and every movement is visible on‑chain.

### **Schedule:**

Locked for 2 months after listing, then released evenly over 12 months (\~4.2M per month).<br>

### **Policy:**

The Treasury is spent on the project, not sold into the pool. If a sale is required for operations, sales in any 7‑day window are capped at 10% of the previous 7 days' trading volume, and every sale is disclosed to the community with its transaction link within 24 hours. The cap is what protects the price; disclosing after the fact rather than before means nobody can trade ahead of the Treasury. The one exception is a major opportunity that requires more — that is put to the community with the reasoning before it is acted on. The Treasury also receives half of the $MTA from every marketplace sale where the maker is paid in cash.
