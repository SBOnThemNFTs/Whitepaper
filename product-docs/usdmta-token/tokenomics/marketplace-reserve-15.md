# Marketplace Reserve 15%

The Marketplace Reserve fuels the marketplace itself: product orders, maker payments and marketplace rewards. These tokens are spent on marketplace fulfilment and nothing else. They are never sold on the open market and never converted to stablecoins or fiat by the project.

### **Schedule:**

3% Unlocked at listing, then released evenly over 12 months (12.125M per month). Released tokens that aren't needed for fulfilment stay in the reserve wallet.<br>

#### **How the marketplace feeds back into $MTA.**&#x20;

Every marketplace purchase is paid in $MTA, which buyers acquire from the pool. Makers are paid in $MTA; a maker who prefers cash is paid from the pool's accumulated trading fees, and the $MTA from that sale is split 50% to the Treasury and 50% burned. Each sale therefore either passes tokens to a maker or removes them from circulation — the marketplace is a source of demand and a source of burns, never a source of sell pressure from the project. <br>

{% hint style="info" %}
**For Highly Exclusive Products Only** - If the merchant explicitly states payment is only accepted in USD or USDC, trading fees accumulated from $MTA will be used and 100% of the token acquired for the order will be burned.
{% endhint %}
